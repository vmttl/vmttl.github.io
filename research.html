Research and Projects

Research

  • MSRI-UP(At Simons Laufer Mathematical Sciences Institute): Six week REU in Berkeley, CA creating a mathematical model representing the daily circadian rhythms of reproductive hormones during the follicular phase of the menstrual cycle. [PDF]

Projects

  • Euler Methods for Lotka-Volterra: Demonstrating through comparison why Symplectic Euler Method is more well suited than Forward or Backward Euler Methods for Hamiltonian systems such as Lotka-Volterra, a pair of first-order nonlinear autonomous differential equations that capture the population dynamics of a predator-prey relationship. [PDF]
  • Sparse Cholesky on Graph Laplacians: Learning and applying a method to solve Graph Lacplacian linear systems by reducing cost through Cholsesky factorization and random vertex deletion, as written by Kyng and Sachdeva. [PDF]
  • UCSB Senior Thesis: Exploration of an analog of the Euclidean Wasserstein-2 distance on the space of finite-dimensional Markov chains. [PDF]
  • UCSB Independent Study: A introductory paper on differential forms.[PDF]
  • UCSB Directed Reading Program: Generating functions and their application to percolation on d-regular trees. [PDF]
